# Dolucies: Chamada Rápida

Build a high-converting, lightning-fast, mobile-first single-page microsite for a local Doceria named Dolucies.

The primary goal of this microsite is to drive immediate phone calls and form submissions from local customers.

Design & Vibe:

Clean, modern, trustworthy aesthetic using a [INSERT COLOR PREFERENCE, e.g., deep navy and safety orange] color palette.

Fully responsive, optimized layout that looks stunning on mobile devices first (large touch targets, readable typography, no horizontal scrolling).

Smooth transitions and a polished, professional finish.

Required Sections & Layout Structure:

Header / Navigation: Logo on the left, phone number click-to-call button on the right, sticky on scroll.

Hero Section: Compelling headline targeting local [INSERT CITY/REGION], strong subheadline, prominent primary CTA button ("Call Now" or "Get a Free Quote"), and a secondary trust-badge element (e.g., "Licensed, Insured & 5-Star Rated").

Trust / Social Proof: A clean strip showing review highlights, Google rating (4.9/5), or local badges.

Core Services Grid: 3-4 distinct cards outlining the primary services offered, complete with icons and short descriptions.

Why Choose Us / Benefits: 3 bulleted advantages highlighting local presence, experience, or guarantees.

Testimonials Carousel: Real customer reviews with names and star ratings.

Interactive Lead Capture / Contact Section: A simple, frictionless contact form (Name, Phone, Service Needed, Message) alongside direct business hours and physical location details.

Footer: Copyright, quick links, license numbers

Make sure all buttons are interactive, forms have basic validation/success states, and the overall code is clean and modular.
